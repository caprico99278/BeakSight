## Important finding

1. **Construction failure can strand a non-terminal guarded Context without a reachable owner or join path** (`src/browser/context-factory.ts:68`, `src/browser/context-factory.ts:75`, `src/browser/context-factory.ts:77`, `src/browser/context-factory.ts:83`, `src/browser/context-factory.ts:87`, `src/browser/context-factory.ts:123`; `src/safety/passive-request-guard.ts:452`; `src/safety/passive-request-guard.ts:458`; `src/safety/passive-request-guard.ts:479`). `createPassiveContext()` does not register the new Context or ledger until after `installPassiveRequestGuard()` fulfills. If installation fails and the Guard's invalidation-time raw `context.close()` also fails, `invalidateContext()` records `GUARD_CONTEXT_INVALIDATION_FAILED`, drains, leaves `PASSIVE_INVALIDATING`, and fulfills its completion; installation then rethrows. The factory creation call rejects without returning the Context and without recording it in `#activeContexts`, so neither the caller nor a factory API can rejoin the published completion. The existing component test at `tests/component/context-factory.test.ts:89` covers only successful raw close and explicitly confirms that the failed Context is not factory-owned at line 106.

   The same reachability gap exists one layer later in `createInteractionSession()`: a Page-readiness failure can retain a failed-close Context in the factory's private `WeakSet`, but lines 87-91 rethrow before returning either the Context or a session. A `WeakSet` cannot be enumerated and the factory has no cleanup/join-all operation, so this nominal internal retention does not provide the binding requirement's “valid join/retry path.” The new parameterized test at `tests/component/context-factory.test.ts:348` begins with a separately returned Context and therefore cannot detect the construction path.

   This violates numbered requirement 6 (a raw invalidation/closing failure must not lose ownership of a non-terminal Context and must retain a valid join path) and requirement 7 (factory/session owner release only after Guard terminal confirmation). Add behavioral REDs for installation failure plus raw-close failure and `createInteractionSession()` readiness failure plus raw-close failure, then retain an externally usable cleanup owner or otherwise guarantee terminal ownership before rejecting construction. Raw close must remain exactly once.

## Numbered lifecycle and ownership adjudication

1. **Active-only invalidation observation — addressed for returned sessions.** `BrowserContextFactory.#requireActiveGuardedContext()` now only checks eligibility and does not delete `#activeContexts` (`src/browser/context-factory.ts:166`). `InteractionGuardedSession.close()` remains callable and sets its local terminal flag only from the Guard query (`src/browser/context-factory.ts:102-110`). The real factory/session regression at `tests/component/context-factory.test.ts:253` proves an intervening active-only rejection cannot make close settle before the admitted evidence cutoff and proves one raw close.
2. **Deterministic successful-invalidation public outcome — addressed.** While invalidating, `closePassiveGuardedContext()` awaits the published completion and throws the canonical invalidated outcome (`src/safety/passive-request-guard.ts:771-783`); after `CLOSED`, the active-state gate produces the same public invalidated message (`src/safety/passive-request-guard.ts:689-697`). `tests/integration/passive-request-guard.test.ts:1442` compares both timings after late task evidence, and the real audit parameterization at `tests/integration/isolated-interaction.test.ts:1133` proves both INVALIDATING and CLOSED observers finalize as `BLOCKED_BY_SAFETY` with one raw close and final cleanup evidence.
3. **`invalidation > normal close` priority — addressed.** Closing-phase invalidation synchronously promotes `PASSIVE_CLOSING`/`FROZEN_CLOSING` to the corresponding invalidating phase and joins the already-published completion without a second raw close (`src/safety/passive-request-guard.ts:438-445`). Normal close publishes that completion before `context.close()` (`src/safety/passive-request-guard.ts:784-796`), detects promotion after stable drain, transitions terminal only after cleanup/drain, and then rejects as invalidated (`src/safety/passive-request-guard.ts:806-819`). The completion has no independent phase/status authority.
4. **VERIFIED work followed by close then invalidation — addressed.** `auditInteraction()` preserves work as a value, waits for session close, snapshots the final ledger afterward, and applies freeze/close-failure precedence without replacing evidence (`src/interaction/isolated-auditor.ts:363-389`). The real regression at `tests/integration/isolated-interaction.test.ts:1247` establishes VERIFIED changed evidence first, invalidates during a gated normal close without a freeze event, and requires `BLOCKED_BY_SAFETY`, preserved work evidence, final invariant evidence, and one raw close.
5. **Order convergence — addressed.** `tests/integration/isolated-interaction.test.ts:1204-1273` exercises both `invalidation -> close` and `close -> invalidation` through the installed Guard and real session/auditor path. It compares final status, the complete Safety Ledger snapshot, changed evidence, and raw-close cardinality; both orders end `BLOCKED_BY_SAFETY` with one raw close.
6. **Failed raw close ownership — partially addressed, but blocked by Important 1.** For an already-returned Context/session, the Guard retains an invalidating phase and one completion, factory deletion is conditional on `CLOSED`, and the session flag remains false (`src/safety/passive-request-guard.ts:793-819`; `src/browser/context-factory.ts:102-110`, `src/browser/context-factory.ts:151-157`). `tests/component/context-factory.test.ts:298` proves later session and factory calls still join, the physical Page remains open, and raw close remains once. Construction-failure paths do not provide the same reachable ownership.
7. **Terminal confirmation before release — partially addressed, but blocked by Important 1.** `isPassiveRequestGuardClosed()` is a read-only query of the single `GuardState.phase` (`src/safety/passive-request-guard.ts:704-706`), not a second lifecycle owner. Factory/session cleanup consumes it only to decide whether to drop their ownership (`src/browser/context-factory.ts:109`, `src/browser/context-factory.ts:145`, `src/browser/context-factory.ts:156`). The Page-close/Page-readiness regression at `tests/component/context-factory.test.ts:348` catches unconditional deletion for a caller-held Context, but not construction rejection where no usable owner is returned.
